Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si Do

The whole secret, in one breath

When you noticed it’s just Do to Si — seven notes that repeat — you found the real thing. Almost every song you love lives inside those seven notes. Nothing more is hiding.

Playing by ear isn’t a gift some people are born with. It’s three small skills stacked together: knowing where the notes are, hearing how far one note jumps to the next, and trusting your hand to follow. We’ll build them one day at a time — and you’ll practice each one on the keyboard right here on this page.

Do one a day. Each takes 15–20 minutes: read the idea, then play the game with the keyboard below.
