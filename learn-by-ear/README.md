# Play What You Hear — a 10-day ear-training tutor for organ

A single-page, offline website that teaches an organ player to **play by ear** — from
"do re mi" all the way to playing songs back from memory — in ten short daily lessons.

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). No install,
no internet needed once loaded — all the sound is generated live in your browser.
Headphones make the ear-training clearer.

- Press **Open the keyboard & games** at the bottom to reveal your playable keyboard.
- Play with your mouse/finger, or your computer keys **A–L** (white notes) and
  **W E T Y U O P** (black notes).
- Do one day at a time. Read the idea, then tap the day's game to practice on the keyboard.
- Your progress (days completed) and label/theme preferences are saved in the browser.

## The ten days

1. Meet the seven notes — where **Do** lives and the map of the keys
2. The staircase — the major scale, Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si Do
3. Match one note — your first real ear-training
4. Steps and skips — the smallest jumps (seconds and thirds)
5. The strong jumps — fourths, fifths, and the octave
6. Up, down, or same — following a melody's shape
7. Your first songs — echo a real tune back by ear
8. Notes that agree — chords for the left hand
9. Find home — hearing which note a song rests on
10. Put it all together — hear it, find it, play it

## Songbook — famous songs to play

Below the ten days there's a **Songbook** of well-known, beginner-friendly tunes written
in Do–Re–Mi (nearly all on the white keys):

- Ode to Joy · Twinkle, Twinkle · Jingle Bells · Mary Had a Little Lamb
- Row, Row, Row Your Boat · When the Saints Go Marching In · Old MacDonald
- Happy Birthday · London Bridge · Für Elise (a "level-up" tune that uses black keys)

For each song you can **click any note** to hear it, press **Play** to hear the whole
tune with the keys lighting up, or press **Learn** to play it back one note at a time.

## What's inside

- A small pipe-organ voice built with the Web Audio API (additive/drawbar synthesis).
- A 2-octave playable keyboard (C4–C6) with fixed-**Do** solfège labels.
- Interactive games for each day: note matching, interval training, melodic direction,
  song echo, chord building, and finding the tonic.
- Light and dark themes; works down to phone-sized screens.
